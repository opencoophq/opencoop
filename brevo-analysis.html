Analysis & Insights

Open rates are exceptional. Coöperanten average 61.3% opens, sympathisanten 53.3%. Industry benchmarks for energy/nonprofit emails sit around 21%. This indicates high trust and brand recognition — people want to hear from Bronsgroen.
Newsletter 2026_01 (C) had 40 hard bounces — 5.6% of sends. This is a hygiene red flag. Hard bounces mean invalid/dead email addresses. These should be purged from the Coöperanten list immediately to protect sender reputation. Subsequent campaigns show 0 hard bounces, so this may have been addressed, but verify.
Newsletter 2026_01 (C) was a monster performer: 78.3% open rate, 33% CTR, 42.2% CTOR. This was the "new dashboard + new website" announcement — high novelty drove exceptional engagement. Hard to replicate but establishes the ceiling.
Newsletter 2026_02 had 0% CTR despite 54.9% opens. Subject: "Save the date: AV op 9 mei". This is an informational-only email with no actionable CTA. Not necessarily bad — but worth checking if a calendar link or RSVP could capture intent.
Lentecampagne E1 Acquisitie underperformed. Only 6 clicks from 390 delivered (1.5% CTR) and 3 unsubscribes. Sympathisanten are colder by definition, but the drop from the Jan newsletter (9.6% CTR) to April Lentecampagne (1.5%) is significant. The subject "Word mede-eigenaar van zonne-energie in Limburg" may feel too salesy for a prospect list. Consider softer approaches for E2–E4.
Coöperanten engage 2–3x more than sympathisanten across every metric. This is expected but the gap on the Lentecampagne is especially stark (3.9% vs 1.5% CTR). The upsell CTA ("Koop extra aandelen") is direct and appropriate for existing shareholders. Prospects need more nurturing before a hard ask.
Brevo link-level tracking shows 0 for all URLs. This is a known Brevo API limitation — campaign-level click counts are accurate, but per-link breakdowns return 0 via API. To see which specific links (register page, project pages, etc.) get clicks, check the Brevo dashboard directly. This matters for understanding whether CTA buttons or inline links drive action.
Testimonial Collection had a solid 7% CTR (47 unique clicks → Microsoft Forms). That's 47 cooperants who clicked through to share their story. If even half completed the form, ~24 testimonials is excellent social proof for E3 (Social proof + mijlpaal).

Recommendations for E2–E5

E2 Projectfocus Genk-Zuid: Lead with the visual impact (renderings, site photos) — project-specific emails haven't been tested yet. Expect higher engagement than generic upsell bc it's concrete and new. Include a dedicated purchase link with the Genk-Zuid project pre-selected: ?class=A&project=cmmd8t3ph000381vam36ppx0e
E3 Social proof: Drop the testimonials collected from campaign 5 here. Real quotes from cooperants >>> marketing copy. If you got the €1M milestone data, lead with that — "samen al €X opgehaald" creates momentum.
E4 Herinnering (laatste kans): For sympathisanten, consider a softer variant. The 3 unsubscribes from E1 suggest the acquisition list is sensitive to pressure. For coöperanten, urgency works — they're already bought in.
E5 AV-uitnodiging: Combine the AV invite with campaign results. "Dankzij u hebben we €X opgehaald in 3 weken" closes the loop. This is also the last email before May 7 — make the final CTA count.
