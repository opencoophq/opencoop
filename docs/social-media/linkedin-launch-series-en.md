# OpenCoop LinkedIn Launch Series (English)

## Post 1 — Launch Announcement (Week 1, Monday)

At Bronsgroen, we needed a proper platform for managing our cooperative. Existing tools were either too expensive or spreadsheets that were falling apart at the seams. So we built our own.

Today, we're opening OpenCoop to cooperatives everywhere.

Here's what it does:

→ Member registry with shareholder portal
→ Bank import with automatic OGM matching
→ Belgian tax calculations (withholding tax)
→ Each cooperative gets its own workspace
→ Deploy as SaaS or self-hosted

The code is open source (AGPL). Pricing and a live demo at opencoop.be.

## Post 2 — Member Registry & Portal (Week 1, Thursday)

Running a 200-member cooperative from a spreadsheet? It works. Until someone accidentally overwrites a row.

OpenCoop gives every cooperative a digital member registry with:

→ Individual members, companies, and minors — all in one place
→ A personal portal where members view their shares, dividends, and documents
→ Online registration with EPC QR code for smooth payments
→ Multiple share classes with their own pricing and voting rights

No more Excel. No more mistakes.

Try the demo at opencoop.be/demo

## Post 3 — Bank Import & OGM Matching (Week 2, Monday)

Every month: download bank statements, manually match OGM codes to registrations, hope there are no typos.

Sound familiar?

OpenCoop automates all of it:

→ Connect your bank account via Ponto Connect (PSD2)
→ Incoming payments are automatically matched to registrations by OGM code
→ When a match happens, the transaction closes automatically
→ No manual work, no errors

From bank statement to confirmed payment — no steps in between.

See how it works at opencoop.be

#cooperative #bankAutomation #PSD2 #openBanking

## Post 4 — Dividends & Tax Compliance (Week 2, Thursday)

Paying dividends sounds straightforward. Until you calculate withholding tax per member, per share class, with different rates.

OpenCoop does the math:

→ Automatic calculation of gross dividend, withholding tax, and net payout
→ Each member gets a clear dividend certificate (PDF)
→ Annual tax overview for compliance
→ Support for multiple dividend periods

Belgian tax law, handled correctly. No spreadsheet gymnastics.

Learn more at opencoop.be

## Post 6 — Messaging (Week 3, Thursday)

Communication with members often happens through scattered emails, newsletters, or — let's be honest — not at all.

OpenCoop puts a built-in inbox in every cooperative:

→ Send messages to individual members or broadcast to everyone
→ Members can reply directly
→ Attach files to messages
→ Email notifications alert members to new messages

One place for all member communication. No more scattered email threads.
