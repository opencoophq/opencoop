## 1. Content Pillars

OpenCoop content on LinkedIn should rotate around these four pillars. Each pillar serves a specific purpose in the customer journey and positions OpenCoop as both a practical tool and a thought leader in cooperative tech.

### Pillar 1: Product Capability (30%)
Highlight specific features and workflows that solve real pain points for cooperative administrators. Show, don't tell.

**Example topics:**
- Automated OGM matching and bank reconciliation
- Dividend calculation with Belgian withholding tax (roerende voorheffing)
- Digital member portal and self-service registration
- Share class management and multi-currency support
- Certificate generation and compliance workflows
- API and integration capabilities

**Tone:** Technical but accessible. Assume the audience manages cooperatives but may not be software experts.

**CTA pattern:** "Try the demo" or "See how we handle X for cooperatives"

---

### Pillar 2: Customer Stories & Social Proof (25%)
Real examples from Bronsgroen and other early customers. Stories are the most effective way to reach cooperative federation leaders.

**Example topics:**
- Bronsgroen case study: how they manage 751+ shareholders without a dedicated IT person
- Scale milestones: member count, transaction volume, processing time improvements
- Admin time saved (before/after metrics)
- Board member testimonials on compliance and reporting
- "How X cooperative uses OpenCoop for Y" variations

**Tone:** Specific numbers and facts. Personal without being cheesy. Lead with the result.

**CTA pattern:** "Ask us for the case study" or "Schedule a call to discuss your use case"

---

### Pillar 3: Thought Leadership & Industry Insights (25%)
Position OpenCoop as an expert on cooperative tech, digitalization trends, and regulatory landscape. Target federation leaders and sustainability professionals.

**Example topics:**
- The state of cooperative digitalization in Belgium/Europe
- How open source principles align with cooperative values
- Regulatory changes affecting cooperatives (GDPR, PSD2, AML)
- The cost of spreadsheet management at scale
- Lessons from other sectors (community energy, agriculture) adopting cooperative models
- Trends in renewable energy cooperatives and shareholder engagement

**Tone:** Research-backed, forward-thinking. Position OpenCoop as part of a larger movement, not just a vendor.

**CTA pattern:** "What's your experience?" (engagement-focused) or link to external resources

---

### Pillar 4: Community & Brand (20%)
Build community around cooperative values, open source culture, and Armlab/OpenCoop brand. Secondary priority but critical for long-term trust.

**Example topics:**
- Announcing open source contributions and GitHub milestones
- Team updates and hiring announcements
- Event attendance (federation conferences, energy symposiums)
- Behind-the-scenes on building for cooperatives
- Celebrating customer wins and community contributions
- Partnerships with other cooperative tech projects

**Tone:** Warm, human, authentic. Respect the cooperative movement.

**CTA pattern:** "Join us on GitHub" or "Get in touch"

## 5. Post Format Guidelines

### Character Limits & Structure

**LinkedIn post character limit:** 3,000 (but engagement drops after 150 characters)

**Optimal format:**
- **Hook (first 150 chars):** Grab attention, answer "Why should I care?"
- **Body (next 500 chars):** Add context, examples, or data
- **CTA (last 100 chars):** Clear, single action
- **Spacing:** Use line breaks generously. Walls of text get skipped.

**Example structure:**
```
[Hook — end with a question or surprising fact]

[Body — 2–4 short paragraphs with specific examples]

[List or visual breakdown if relevant]

[CTA — one clear action]

[Hashtags]
```

### Hashtag Strategy

**Always include 5–8 hashtags.** Mix broad and niche:

**Broad (for reach):**
- #cooperative #cooperatives #coöperatie
- #opensource #fintech #saas

**Niche (for relevance):**
- #energycooperative #renewableenergy #Rescoop
- #belgiantech #europeantech #solidarityeconomy
- #ledenbeheer #cooperatief #coöperatief

**Trending (when relevant):**
- #InternationalCooperativesDay (first Saturday in July)
- #EuropeanCooperativesWeek (October)

**Avoid:** #marketing #business #innovation (too generic, killed by algorithm)

### Media Recommendations

- **Featured image:** Required for ~40% higher engagement. Use OpenCoop dashboard screenshots (anonymized), Bronsgroen logo, or custom graphics.
- **Dimensions:** 1200 x 627px (LinkedIn optimal)
- **File format:** JPG or PNG, <10MB
- **Style:** Clean, branded, readable on mobile. Avoid stock photos.
- **Video:** Short clips (15–60 sec) of platform walkthroughs or feature demos. Subtitles required.

### Carousels (Multi-Image Posts)

Use for:
- Step-by-step workflows (e.g., "How to set up OpenCoop")
- Before/after scenarios (spreadsheet → OpenCoop)
- Feature comparisons
- Case study highlights

**Best practice:** 3–5 slides max. Text-heavy images. Include CTA on final slide.

### CTA Patterns

**Pillar 1 (Product):** "Try the demo on opencoop.be" or "Ask us about [feature]"
**Pillar 2 (Social Proof):** "Download the case study" or "Schedule a call to discuss your setup"
**Pillar 3 (Thought Leadership):** "What's your take?" or "Share your experience in comments"
**Pillar 4 (Community):** "Check us out on GitHub" or "Follow for updates"

## 6. Measurement

Track these KPIs monthly. OpenCoop currently has limited LinkedIn follower base (targeting federation accounts and early adopters), so focus on engagement quality over vanity metrics.

### Primary KPIs (Track Weekly)

| Metric | Goal | Why | Target |
|--------|------|-----|--------|
| **Engagement Rate** | 4–6% per post | Shows relevance to audience | If below 2%, revisit content angle |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | 1–3% | Conversion signal for demos/case studies | Track via UTM params (opencoop.be/?source=linkedin) |
| **Share/Retweet Rate** | 0.5–1% | Sign of strong message resonance | Thought leadership posts should hit this |
| **Save Rate** | 0.5–1% | Indicates useful, reference-worthy content | Feature posts should encourage saves |

### Secondary KPIs (Track Monthly)

| Metric | Goal | Why | Target |
|--------|------|-----|--------|
| **Follower Growth** | 5–10% per month | Audience expansion | Both OpenCoop + Wouter personal accounts |
| **Demo Signups from LinkedIn** | 2–4 per month | Direct revenue signal | UTM tracking required |
| **Sales Conversations Sourced** | 1–2 per month | B2B conversion signal | Note in CRM which posts drove meetings |
| **Federation Outreach Response** | 20%+ reply rate | Measure thought leadership impact | Track federation leader replies |

### Vanity Metrics (Track But Don't Optimize For)

- Total impressions (varies by follower count; not actionable)
- Likes (less valuable than comments or shares)
- Reach (useful only to detect throttling)

### Quarterly Review

Every 3 months, review:
1. Which pillar drove most engagement? Double down.
2. Which post types underperformed? Adjust or pivot.
3. Any new customer segments appearing in comments? Note for targeting.
4. Are we hitting our demo signup targets? If not, test new CTAs.

## 8. Distribution Checklist

Before publishing any post, verify:

- [ ] **Pillar assignment:** Does it fit one of the 4 pillars?
- [ ] **Language:** Correct language for the account; spell-check completed
- [ ] **Tone:** Warm, confident, respectful of cooperative movement? No corporate jargon?
- [ ] **Data/claims:** Any numbers or facts cited? Are they accurate?
- [ ] **Hook:** Does the first 150 chars grab attention?
- [ ] **CTA:** Is there a single, clear action? (Not 3 things to do)
- [ ] **Hashtags:** 5–8 relevant hashtags included? (No more than 30 chars combined)
- [ ] **Media:** Image attached (if not video/engagement post)? Size 1200 x 627px? Optimized?
- [ ] **Links:** Any URLs are UTM-tagged for tracking? (e.g., `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=launch`)
- [ ] **Schedule:** Posted during optimal hours (Tue–Thu, 8am–11am CET)?
- [ ] **Cross-post:** Relevant for other accounts? (E.g., product posts may work on Armlab too)

## 9. Quick Reference: Tone & Voice

**Do:**
- Be direct. "Manage your shareholders" not "leverage stakeholder engagement synergies."
- Use specific examples. "Bronsgroen manages 751 members" not "We help large cooperatives."
- Show data. Numbers build credibility with federation leaders.
- Respect the movement. Cooperatives are built on trust; honor that.
- Lead with value. "Save 10 hours/month" before talking about features.

**Don't:**
- Use jargon. Assume your audience manages coops, not tech.
- Make big claims without proof. "Easiest platform ever" is weak; "Reduces admin time by 40%" is strong.
- Oversell. Cooperatives value authenticity.
- Go salesy. OpenCoop is a tool, not a lifestyle brand.
- Ignore replies. LinkedIn posts with replies get 5–10x more reach. Respond to comments.
