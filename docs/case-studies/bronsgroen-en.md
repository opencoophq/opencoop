# Bronsgroen: from spreadsheets to fully digital cooperative management

## About Bronsgroen

Bronsgroen is a Flemish energy cooperative investing in renewable energy. With 16 projects — wind turbines and solar panels across Flanders — and 751 shareholders, Bronsgroen manages a substantial share register, annual dividend distributions, and ongoing communication with its members.

## The challenge

Like many energy cooperatives, Bronsgroen managed its member registry for years using spreadsheets and scattered files. With 751 shareholders and nearly 1,000 register entries, it became unmanageable:

- **Manual share register** — every share, transaction, and exit tracked separately in Excel. One wrong row and the numbers no longer add up.
- **Payment matching via OGM codes** — downloading bank statements every month, manually matching Belgian structured communications to subscriptions, hoping there are no typos.
- **Dividend calculations** — computing gross dividend, 30% withholding tax, and net payout per shareholder across multiple years (since 2012). With different entry dates and savings plans, complexity grows quickly.
- **No shareholder portal** — members couldn't view their own participation, dividends, or documents. Every question meant extra work for the board.
- **Communication via scattered emails** — no central channel for member messages.

The board spent hours every month on administrative tasks that should have been automated.

## The solution

Bronsgroen migrated to OpenCoop — a platform built for Belgian cooperatives. The full migration included:

### Data migration
- **751 shareholders** imported with all historical data: personal details, contact information, bank details
- **994 register entries** transferred — every purchase, sale, and exit from the past correctly migrated
- **16 projects** created with their respective investments
- **Dividend history since 2012** fully reconstructed, including year-by-year calculations

### The platform in use

**Share register**
One central source of truth for all shares. Individual shareholders, companies, and minors — each with their own profile, share class (Share A, €125/unit), and transaction history.

**Automatic OGM matching**
Via Ponto Connect (PSD2), incoming bank payments are automatically matched to open subscriptions based on the structured communication code. No manual work required.

**Dividend management**
The annual 1% dividend distribution is calculated automatically per shareholder, including Belgium's 30% withholding tax. Every shareholder receives a PDF dividend statement.

**Shareholder portal**
Members log in and view their participation, transactions, dividends, and documents. New shareholders register online with an EPC QR code for easy payment.

**Messaging**
The board communicates directly with individual shareholders or sends messages to all members. Shareholders can reply — everything in one place.

**Documents**
Share certificates, purchase confirmations, dividend statements, and annual overviews — automatically generated as PDF, available for both the board and shareholders.
