## Slide 2: The problem

### Your member cooperatives still manage shareholders in spreadsheets

Most energy cooperatives in Belgium track their shareholders, shares, and dividends in Excel — or in legacy systems that cost too much and do too little.

**What we hear from cooperative boards:**

- "Our share register is a spreadsheet with 500+ rows. One mistake and we spend hours fixing it."
- "Matching bank payments to OGM codes takes a full afternoon every month."
- "Calculating dividends with withholding tax across different entry dates? We dread it."
- "Our members can't see their own participation. Every question comes to the board."

These are volunteer board members spending their evenings on administration instead of on their cooperative's mission.

> **Speaker notes:** These pain points come directly from working with Bronsgroen. They are universal across Belgian energy coops. The audience will recognize them immediately.

## Slide 3: Introducing OpenCoop

### One platform for everything a Belgian cooperative needs

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Share register** | Central registry for all shareholders, share classes, and transactions |
| **Shareholder portal** | Members view their participation, dividends, and documents online |
| **OGM payment matching** | Automatic bank reconciliation via Ponto Connect (PSD2) |
| **Dividend management** | Automatic calculation with 30% withholding tax (roerende voorheffing) |
| **Document generation** | Share certificates, dividend statements, annual reports — all as PDF |
| **Messaging** | Built-in communication between board and members |
| **Multi-language** | Dutch, French, English, German |
| **Custom branding** | Each cooperative gets its own look and feel |

> **Speaker notes:** Walk through each feature briefly. Emphasize the Belgian-specific elements: OGM codes, withholding tax, IBAN validation, KBO numbers. These are not afterthoughts — they are core to the platform.

## Slide 5: Why open source matters

### Built on cooperative principles

OpenCoop is licensed under AGPL-3.0. The source code is publicly available on GitHub.

**What this means for cooperatives:**

- **No vendor lock-in** — your data is yours. If you ever want to leave, you can.
- **Full transparency** — see exactly how your data is processed. No black boxes.
- **Self-hosting option** — run it on your own servers if you prefer. We support that.
- **Community-driven** — cooperatives helping cooperatives. Contributions are welcome.

**Why this matters for federations:**
Recommending a proprietary SaaS tool carries risk. If the vendor raises prices or shuts down, your members are stuck. With open source, the software exists independently of any single company.

> **Speaker notes:** This resonates strongly with cooperative values. Federations are careful about endorsing commercial products. Open source removes the biggest objection: dependency on a single vendor.
